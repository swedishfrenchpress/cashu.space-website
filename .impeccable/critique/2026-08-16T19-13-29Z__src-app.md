## Priority Issues

### [P0 — FIXED] Homepage scrolled horizontally into a blank page below 1024px
`.sr-only` inside `.press-card` was `position: absolute` with no positioned ancestor, escaping the track's `overflow-x: auto` clip. Stray scroll of 316–979px at every width 320–1023. Also a WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.10 (Reflow) failure at 400% zoom. Introduced by the new-tab-hints commit. Fixed with `position: relative` on `.press-card`; verified 0 at all 13 widths.

### [P1 — FIXED] Hero CTAs fell below the fold on common laptops
`.hero-spec` padding was two pure `vw` clamps with no height term; every Fold-Line amendment had been measured only at 1440×900. At 1366×665 that was 287px of padding in a 665px fold and both CTAs were sliced; at 1280×600 the row was entirely below the fold. Now `min(9vw, 10svh)` / `min(12vw, 14svh)`. Reference composition untouched (137/180 → 140/176).

### [P1 — FIXED] Footer disclaimer at 4.29:1
Under AA for 16px on the bloom's hotspot at 390px. DESIGN.md recorded 5.32:1, which sampled the modal ground rather than the worst. Now Chalk: 4.96:1 at the peak, 13.9:1 typical.

### [P1 — OPEN] The OG card is ungoverned and violates four rules
`public/og-image.jpg`: full-colour stock photography of US $100 bills, rounded-corner screenshots, green `+₿` amounts. Breaks the No-Colour Rule, §6's stock-photography ban, the Signal-Green Exception's explicit "no green success state", and the sharp-corner doctrine. It is the site's first impression in every share — seen by more people than the site — and DESIGN.md does not mention it anywhere. **Fix:** replace with a Paper/Ink type card in the hero's own rag, then add an "Open Graph card" entry to §5 so it is governed.
