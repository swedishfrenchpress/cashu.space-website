## Design Context

This project has a documented design system. Before generating UI, styling components, or making visual choices, read:

- **[PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md)** — strategic spec: register (`brand`), users, brand personality, anti-references, design principles.
- **[DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md)** — visual spec: tokens, type scale, components, named rules, do's and don'ts. Follows the [Stitch DESIGN.md format](https://stitch.withgoogle.com/docs/design-md/format/).
- **[.impeccable/design.json](.impeccable/design.json)** — machine-readable sidecar with tonal ramps, motion, breakpoints, and component HTML/CSS snippets.

**North Star:** *"The Open Specification"* — cashu.space dresses an open protocol in the visual register of a published RFC. Monochrome by doctrine, GT-Standard carries the page, Geist Pixel Square reserved for protocol notation (amounts, mint ids, version strings).

**Hard rules (see DESIGN.md for the full set):**

- Greys shift by lightness, never by hue. Colour exists in exactly two places, both named in DESIGN.md §2: third-party marks (Depicted-World Exception) and `--signal` green on the protocol-parts property squares (Signal-Green Exception, user-directed 2026-08-16). The green is a scoped licence for one 7px decorative square on the always-dark column — not type, not a state, not a hover, not a palette slot. Don't spread it, and don't open a third.
- No `box-shadow` anywhere — buttons included; they are flat cipher slabs. There is **no exception**: the navbar's condensed box was the one licence and it was retired 2026-08-16 (DESIGN.md §4–5).
- No gradient text, no side-stripe borders, and **no glass anywhere** — blur and translucency have no sanctioned surface left on the site since the navbar's condensed box went.
- **The navbar is a full-bleed masthead** (`site-header.tsx`, `nav-clock.tsx`, DESIGN.md §5 Navigation), rebuilt 2026-08-16 on the user's direction against a layout reference. Ink ground, identical in every scroll state: a brand plate hard against the left edge, then one uninterrupted ink run to the right edge carrying a `HH:MM UTC` Geist Mono clock, the links, and the marks. **The plate follows the scheme** — `--nav-plate` is `--paper` and its text is `--ink`, so it is the page punched through the bar, not a fixed white slab; a hardcoded `#ffffff` there is the version the user rejected. Hovering a link (or the current route at rest) drives that same plate across it with `clip-path` over 460ms — the label is rendered twice so the wipe edge passes through the letterforms. The clock updates on the minute, never per second — a per-second field is the animated ticker the anti-references rule out.
- Three things the navbar tried and lost, all user-directed 2026-08-16, none of which should come back: the **Onyx two-state condense** (with `--nav-inset`, `--nav-condensed-max`, `--nav-shadow`, the `--glass-*` ramp, the glass, the radius and the shadow), the **slot** (an open column mid-bar; it read as a hole punched in the bar, worst over the hero), and the **dither strip** (a pixel checkerboard along the bottom edge; it read as noise under the bar). The bar meets the page on a clean line and the value step does the separating.
- Three typefaces only — GT-Standard (everything readable), Geist Mono (technical), Geist Pixel Square (protocol notation). No third.
- Hero headline must be Display: GT-Standard 600, ≥3.75rem, line-height 0.95.
- **The hero has no ground, and it sets once and then holds** (the Set-Once Rule, DESIGN.md §4; added 2026-08-16 on the user's direction). Its entire motion is the arrival: the closing hairline draws across the fold (`hero-rule-draw` in `globals.css`, `--dur-draw-on`), the word *ecash* resolves out of hex (`hero-cipher.tsx`, 760ms, decrypt-only), and the copy settles on the staged `Reveal`. By ~1.1s nothing moves, and nothing moves again. **No ambient loop, no idle cycle, no looping canvas, no hover response** — don't reintroduce one by increments. The title block is **centred in the section** (`.hero-spec__inner { margin-block: auto }`, asymmetric section padding for an optical lift of ~5%), because with no figure the old "title at the top" left 40% of the fold as dead ground; the closing hairline is the horizon now. Read the **Fold-Line Rule's fourth amendment** — the first that changes that rule's substance — before touching the hero's vertical composition.
- **What the hero lost, and what may go back.** `hero-orb.tsx` and `src/lib/orb/` are deleted: the wide dot figure was a `thinking-orbs` blob, which is PRODUCT.md's second anti-reference in silhouette, and it depicted nothing. `src/lib/ascii/` and `ascii-field.tsx` went before it (`terrain.ts` and `vault.ts` were pinned by parity fixtures in cashubtc/wallet, which now have no counterpart here — **a coordination item with that repo**). If a figure ever returns, the bar is the Honest-Network Rule's **first** branch — it must depict real protocol structure — and it should advance on scroll or arrival rather than run a clock. The strongest candidate is the **blind-signature round trip**, still recoverable at `git show 5e4c0e8^:src/lib/ascii/bdhke.ts`. If you ever need a ₿ anywhere: **GT-Standard's shipped trial faces cover 271 codepoints and have no U+20BF** (nor `$ ¥ €`), and neither does Geist Mono, so `fillText("₿")` silently renders a system font or tofu — it has to be drawn.
- The **cipher pass** is the site's one typographic motion primitive and now lives in `src/lib/cipher.ts`, shared by two consumers: `button-cipher.tsx` (encrypt→decrypt, on hover, 460ms) and `hero-cipher.tsx` (decrypt-only, once, on arrival). Both create their overlay in an effect and remove it when done — never leave it in the DOM, because contrast extensions assign backgrounds by computed value and turn a permanent invisible overlay into a visible plate. Both bail entirely under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- The homepage's middle is one full-bleed 40/60 split band (`protocol-parts.tsx`, DESIGN.md §5 The Split Spec Sheet), added 2026-08-16 on the user's direction. It replaced the four-parts feature scroller, the tap-to-pay video, and the properties bento in one move; the video, `protocol-demo*.tsx`, the `--fig-*` tokens, and the Demo Panel signature are all deleted. It is deliberately outside the page shell — the entry row needs the width — so don't "align" it back to `--page-max`. Each entry's plate is a `thinking-orbs` canvas driven through the library's engine at ~280px, not its `<ThinkingOrb>` component (which ships only 64px and 20px); see `orb-figure.tsx`. The Honest-Network Rule was amended for these plates: abstract orbs are material and pass freely, but `connecting` is a topology and is pinned to the Mints entry alone, where Lightning makes a mesh true. Don't move it to another entry or add a second instance. (The hero used to run `listening` and no longer runs anything — these four plates are the only `thinking-orbs` figures left on the site.)
- **The homepage hero is centred on purpose. Everything else on the site is left-aligned.** This is the one deliberate departure from the editorial register, added 2026-08-14 on the user's direction — do not "restore" it as a consistency fix, and do not spread centring to the sections beneath it. Two things depend on it and move together if it ever reverts: the absence of optical-left corrections (headline −0.058em, deck −0.095em) and the headline's escape from the page shell. The third — "the figure moves off-axis" — was struck 2026-08-16; there is no figure. See the Centred-Hero Exception in DESIGN.md §4.

When in doubt, run `/impeccable critique` against the surface in question before shipping.
