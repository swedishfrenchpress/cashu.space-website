## Design Context

This project has a documented design system. Before generating UI, styling components, or making visual choices, read:

- **[PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md)** — strategic spec: register (`brand`), users, brand personality, anti-references, design principles.
- **[DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md)** — visual spec: tokens, type scale, components, named rules, do's and don'ts. Follows the [Stitch DESIGN.md format](https://stitch.withgoogle.com/docs/design-md/format/).
- **[.impeccable/design.json](.impeccable/design.json)** — machine-readable sidecar with tonal ramps, motion, breakpoints, and component HTML/CSS snippets.

**North Star:** *"The Open Specification"* — cashu.space dresses an open protocol in the visual register of a published RFC. Monochrome by doctrine, GT-Standard carries the page, Geist Pixel Square reserved for protocol notation (amounts, mint ids, version strings).

**Hard rules (see DESIGN.md for the full set):**

- Greys shift by lightness, never by hue. Colour exists in exactly two places, both named in DESIGN.md §2: third-party marks (Depicted-World Exception) and `--signal` green on the protocol-parts property squares (Signal-Green Exception, user-directed 2026-08-16). The green is a scoped licence for one 7px decorative square on the always-dark column — not type, not a state, not a hover, not a palette slot. Don't spread it, and don't open a third.
- No `box-shadow` anywhere — buttons included; they are flat cipher slabs. There is **no exception**: the navbar's condensed box was the one licence and it was retired 2026-08-16 (DESIGN.md §4–5).
- No gradient text, no side-stripe borders, and **no glass anywhere** — blur and translucency have no sanctioned surface left on the site since the navbar's condensed box went.
- **The navbar is a full-bleed masthead** (`site-header.tsx`, `nav-clock.tsx`, DESIGN.md §5 Navigation), rebuilt 2026-08-16 on the user's direction against a layout reference. Ink ground, identical in every scroll state: a brand plate hard against the left edge, then one uninterrupted ink run to the right edge carrying a `HH:MM UTC` Geist Mono clock, the links, and the marks. **The plate follows the scheme** — `--nav-plate` is `--paper` and its text is `--ink`, so it is the page punched through the bar, not a fixed white slab; a hardcoded `#ffffff` there is the version the user rejected. Hovering a link (or the current route at rest) drives that same plate across it with `clip-path` over 460ms — the label is rendered twice so the wipe edge passes through the letterforms. The clock updates on the minute, never per second — a per-second field is the animated ticker the anti-references rule out.
- Three things the navbar tried and lost, all user-directed 2026-08-16, none of which should come back: the **Onyx two-state condense** (with `--nav-inset`, `--nav-condensed-max`, `--nav-shadow`, the `--glass-*` ramp, the glass, the radius and the shadow), the **slot** (an open column mid-bar; it read as a hole punched in the bar, worst over the hero), and the **dither strip** (a pixel checkerboard along the bottom edge; it read as noise under the bar). The bar meets the page on a clean line and the value step does the separating.
- Three typefaces only — GT-Standard (everything readable), Geist Mono (technical), Geist Pixel Square (protocol notation). No third.
- Hero headline must be Display: GT-Standard 600, ≥3.75rem, line-height 0.95.
- The hero's ground is a **wide dot figure**, not an ASCII field (`hero-orb.tsx`, `src/lib/orb/`) — replaced 2026-08-16 on the user's direction. It drives `thinking-orbs`' **`listening`** mode through **`MODE_FRAMES`** (geometry, not `MODE_DRAWS`) at a fixed `GEOM_SIZE`, then remaps the dot positions into an ellipse roughly twice as wide as it is tall **while leaving every radius alone** — the marks stay round, so it reads as a wide field of dots and not as a squashed ball. It is a band across the **bottom third**; filling the hero was tried first and put the ring stack behind the headline, where it read as oversized. It runs its own clock, not the preset's: the library's baked speed is tuned for a 64px avatar and strobes at hero width. **The field contains no figure.** A Bitcoin mark surfaced out of it on a 72s cycle for a few hours on 2026-08-16 and was then removed on the user's direction, along with `btc-glyph.ts`, the reveal cycle, the coverage-clearing pass and the measurement of `.hero-spec__content` that staged it — so the ground depicts nothing at all and the component reaches into no other component's markup. Read the Honest-Network Rule before putting anything back in it. If you ever do need a ₿ anywhere: **GT-Standard's shipped trial faces cover 271 codepoints and have no U+20BF** (nor `$ ¥ €`), and neither does Geist Mono, so `fillText("₿")` silently renders a system font or tofu — it has to be drawn. Two DESIGN.md §4 rules moved with the figure (**Fold-Line**, **Honest-Network** — the latter's "the hero is untouched" clause is retracted there, and what that cost is recorded); read both before changing the hero's geometry, mask, or cycle. `src/lib/ascii/` and `ascii-field.tsx` are deleted; `terrain.ts` and `vault.ts` were pinned by parity fixtures in cashubtc/wallet, which now have no counterpart here — **a coordination item with that repo.**
- The homepage's middle is one full-bleed 40/60 split band (`protocol-parts.tsx`, DESIGN.md §5 The Split Spec Sheet), added 2026-08-16 on the user's direction. It replaced the four-parts feature scroller, the tap-to-pay video, and the properties bento in one move; the video, `protocol-demo*.tsx`, the `--fig-*` tokens, and the Demo Panel signature are all deleted. It is deliberately outside the page shell — the entry row needs the width — so don't "align" it back to `--page-max`. Each entry's plate is a `thinking-orbs` canvas driven through the library's engine at ~280px, not its `<ThinkingOrb>` component (which ships only 64px and 20px); see `orb-figure.tsx`. The Honest-Network Rule was amended for these plates: abstract orbs are material and pass freely, but `connecting` is a topology and is pinned to the Mints entry alone, where Lightning makes a mesh true. Don't move it to another entry or add a second instance — **the hero included**, which runs `listening` (see the hero bullet above).
- **The homepage hero is centred on purpose. Everything else on the site is left-aligned.** This is the one deliberate departure from the editorial register, added 2026-08-14 on the user's direction — do not "restore" it as a consistency fix, and do not spread centring to the sections beneath it. The figure's centred stage, the absence of optical-left corrections, and the headline's escape from the page shell all depend on it; reverting means moving all three together. See the Centred-Hero Exception in DESIGN.md §4.

When in doubt, run `/impeccable critique` against the surface in question before shipping.
