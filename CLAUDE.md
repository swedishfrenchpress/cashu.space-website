## Design Context

This project has a documented design system. Before generating UI, styling components, or making visual choices, read:

- **[PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md)** — strategic spec: register (`brand`), users, brand personality, anti-references, design principles.
- **[DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md)** — visual spec: tokens, type scale, components, named rules, do's and don'ts. Follows the [Stitch DESIGN.md format](https://stitch.withgoogle.com/docs/design-md/format/).
- **[.impeccable/design.json](.impeccable/design.json)** — machine-readable sidecar with tonal ramps, motion, breakpoints, and component HTML/CSS snippets.

**North Star:** *"The Open Specification"* — cashu.space dresses an open protocol in the visual register of a published RFC. Monochrome by doctrine, GT-Standard carries the page, Geist Pixel Square reserved for protocol notation (amounts, mint ids, version strings).

**Hard rules (see DESIGN.md for the full set):**

- Greys shift by lightness, never by hue. Colour exists in exactly two places, both named in DESIGN.md §2: third-party marks (Depicted-World Exception) and the signal green (Signal-Green Exception, user-directed 2026-08-16). The green has **exactly two licensed consumers**, both the same 7px square opening a stated property: the protocol-parts column, and the wallet-directory status tag. It is never type, a fill, a glow, a gradient, a hover, a focus ring, or a success/error state. Two grounds, so two tokens: `--signal` (`#6fe3a4`) on the always-dark column, which never flips, and `--signal-page` on the page ground, which does (`#6fe3a4` is 1.59:1 on Paper — invisible; light mode takes the same hue down to `#1b9d55`). **Scale is the whole exception**: its largest instance anywhere is 7 pixels square. It was briefly given the hero's pointer plume on 2026-08-16 and taken back the same day — at hero scale a borrowed mint accent read as the pastel/Web3 register PRODUCT.md rules out, and `--signal` itself came from aspensearch.com. If a third consumer is ever proposed, weigh it by area first.
- No `box-shadow` anywhere — buttons included; they are flat cipher slabs. There is **no exception**: the navbar's condensed box was the one licence and it was retired 2026-08-16 (DESIGN.md §4–5).
- **No `border-radius` in the site's own voice either.** The wallet directory's `Beta` badge was the last rounded surface — a grey `999px` capsule, the Soft-Capsule Exception of 2026-08-14 — and on 2026-08-16 the user directed it into the signal-square family instead: no fill, no border, no radius, a 7px `--signal-page` square then the word in 12px Geist Mono uppercase tracked 0.06em (DESIGN.md §5 Status Tag). Don't put the capsule back, and note the old comment's argument is now *for* this version, not against it — the SaaS-chip tell was "outline + caps + tracking", and there is no container here at all. Keep `STATUS_FACTS` small: the square distinguishes the maturity fact only while it's the one fact carrying one.
- No gradient text, no side-stripe borders, and **no glass anywhere** — blur and translucency have no sanctioned surface left on the site since the navbar's condensed box went.
- **The navbar is a full-bleed masthead** (`site-header.tsx`, `nav-clock.tsx`, DESIGN.md §5 Navigation), rebuilt 2026-08-16 on the user's direction against a layout reference. Ink ground, identical in every scroll state: a brand plate hard against the left edge, then one uninterrupted ink run to the right edge carrying a `HH:MM UTC` Geist Mono clock, the links, and the marks. **The plate follows the scheme** — `--nav-plate` is `--paper` and its text is `--ink`, so it is the page punched through the bar, not a fixed white slab; a hardcoded `#ffffff` there is the version the user rejected. Hovering a link (or the current route at rest) drives that same plate across it with `clip-path` over 460ms — the label is rendered twice so the wipe edge passes through the letterforms. The clock updates on the minute, never per second — a per-second field is the animated ticker the anti-references rule out.
- Three things the navbar tried and lost, all user-directed 2026-08-16, none of which should come back: the **Onyx two-state condense** (with `--nav-inset`, `--nav-condensed-max`, `--nav-shadow`, the `--glass-*` ramp, the glass, the radius and the shadow), the **slot** (an open column mid-bar; it read as a hole punched in the bar, worst over the hero), and the **dither strip** (a pixel checkerboard along the bottom edge; it read as noise under the bar). The bar meets the page on a clean line and the value step does the separating.
- Three typefaces only — GT-Standard (everything readable), Geist Mono (technical), Geist Pixel Square (protocol notation). No third.
- Hero headline must be Display: GT-Standard 600, ≥3.75rem, line-height 0.95.
- **The hero sets once and then holds, and its only other motion is the one the reader causes** (the Set-Once Rule and its first amendment, DESIGN.md §4; both 2026-08-16, both user-directed). The arrival is: the closing hairline draws across the fold (`hero-rule-draw` in `globals.css`, `--dur-draw-on`), the word *ecash* resolves out of hex (`hero-cipher.tsx`, 760ms, decrypt-only), and the copy settles on the staged `Reveal`. By ~1.1s nothing moves. **No ambient loop, no idle cycle, no timer, no parallax** — don't reintroduce one by increments. The title block is **centred in the section** (`.hero-spec__inner { margin-block: auto }`, asymmetric section padding for an optical lift of ~5%); the closing hairline is the horizon. Read the **Fold-Line Rule's fourth amendment** before touching the hero's vertical composition.
- **The hero's ground is `hero-field.tsx` + `src/lib/hero-field/`** (2026-08-16, user-directed, after aspensearch.com): a frozen field of Geist Mono hex whose occupancy is Perlin noise, with a **monochrome** wake the pointer stirs through it. **Two alphabets:** hex at rest (entropy — what a Cashu secret is), **currency marks inside the wake** (`₿` weighted 6-in-16, then `$ € ¥ ₩ £ ₹ ₽ ¢ ₱ ₴`), set in `--body`, re-encrypting to `--ghost` behind it. Nothing cycles — each cell has one resting digit and one denomination, both hashes of its coordinates; churning was tried and reads as a slot machine. Hand-rolled WebGL2, ~15KB gzipped. **`₿` (U+20BF) and `₩` (U+20A9) are `.notdef` in Geist Mono and are DRAWN** (`glyphs.ts`): `B` plus four rules clear of the letter, `W` crossed by two. `fillText("₿")` silently falls through to a system font — don't 'simplify' the synthesis away, and don't trust `document.fonts.check` (next/font emits no `unicode-range`, so it reports loadedness, not coverage). **It shipped that afternoon as a Bayer dither with a mint-green fluid plume and was rebuilt the same evening, user-directed, for being too close a copy of the reference** — that first version was a port of Aspen's hero down to its solver constants, and with the masthead clock and the split band it made three borrowings from one source in a day. **The gesture survives, the material does not.** Don't reintroduce the halftone or put green back here. **Four properties are the terms on which the Set-Once Rule readmitted a ground at all — breaking any one breaks the rule, it doesn't extend it:** (1) the field never drifts — there is no `time` uniform in `shaders.ts` and there must not be one; (2) there is **no rAF at rest** — the loop starts on `pointermove`, stops ~2.2s after the last one, and `window.__heroFieldFrames` exists so you can prove it; (3) it stops on a frame bit-identical to the resting one; (4) **the solver** is never allocated under `prefers-reduced-motion` or without a fine hover pointer, but the **ground** mounts for everyone — it is a still image, and withholding it would give every phone a different hero. Also: falloffs multiply **occupancy, not alpha** (fading type with alpha greys the letterforms into a smudge — the ASCII field's lesson); the mask fits the **union of `.hero-spec__content`'s children**, not the block, which is full-width and would clear the whole hero; **`WAKE_OCCUPANCY_GAIN` must stay below `OCCUPANCY`** or every cell the wake touches clears the threshold and the disturbance renders as a solid block pasted over the ground; **the field's density MUST be measured at more than one viewport** — the safe box tracks the width-capped type, so a bigger window adds field rather than clearing more of it, and the same build measured 10.4% of cells occupied at 1440×900 against 35.4% at 1920×1200. That is why `SAFE_FEATHER` is a *fraction* of the box-to-canvas gap rather than pixels, and why `MIN_SAFE_FRAC_X/Y` floor the cleared zone at a fraction of the canvas. Shipped: 8.4% / 11.3%. Re-measure both viewports when touching any density constant; the per-scheme gain is **square-rooted**, an empirical damping, because coverage is not linear in gain at a given threshold; the section's top/bottom edges get their own fade or the densest band lands under the masthead and reinstates the rejected `--nav-edge` strip; nothing renders before `document.fonts.ready` or the atlas rasterises in the system monospace; the alphabet is hex and **never words** — legible copy here is an assertion nobody wrote. **Don't add Three.js/R3F**; the reference pays ~1.1MB for what two triangles and four render targets do here. The field stands on the Honest-Network Rule's *second* branch only, recorded honestly in §4, and is not a licence for a **figure** made of noise.
- **What the hero lost, and what may go back.** `hero-orb.tsx` and `src/lib/orb/` are deleted: the wide dot figure was a `thinking-orbs` blob, which is PRODUCT.md's second anti-reference in silhouette, and it depicted nothing. `src/lib/ascii/` and `ascii-field.tsx` went before it (`terrain.ts` and `vault.ts` were pinned by parity fixtures in cashubtc/wallet, which now have no counterpart here — **a coordination item with that repo**). The hero has a **ground** again (the cipher field, above) but still no **figure**, and the distinction is load-bearing: a ground is stock and owes no assertion, a figure occupies attention and does. So the bar for a figure is unmoved — the Honest-Network Rule's **first** branch, depicting real protocol structure, advancing on scroll or arrival rather than running a clock. The strongest candidate is still the **blind-signature round trip**, recoverable at `git show 5e4c0e8^:src/lib/ascii/bdhke.ts`. If you ever need a ₿ anywhere: **GT-Standard's shipped trial faces cover 271 codepoints and have no U+20BF** (nor `$ ¥ €`), and neither does Geist Mono, so `fillText("₿")` silently renders a system font or tofu — it has to be drawn.
- The **cipher pass** is the site's one typographic motion primitive and now lives in `src/lib/cipher.ts`, shared by two consumers: `button-cipher.tsx` (encrypt→decrypt, on hover, 460ms) and `hero-cipher.tsx` (decrypt-only, once, on arrival). **The hazard both guard against is the same and their answers differ**, so don't "unify" them: contrast extensions assign backgrounds by computed value, and a permanently invisible overlay left in the DOM becomes a visible plate. `hero-cipher.tsx` runs once, so it creates its overlay and **removes** it when the pass settles (measured: zero nodes left). `button-cipher.tsx` re-runs on every hover, so it creates the overlay at enhance time and **hides** it between passes — `display: none` except under `.button-ciphering` (`globals.css`), measured 0×0 — removing it only on unmount. Hiding is what closes the hazard there; re-creating a node per pointerenter would not. Both bail entirely under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

`button-cipher.tsx` reads **visible text only** when it builds its scramble source — it skips `.sr-only` and its own overlay. A CTA carrying `<NewTabHint />` would otherwise paint "READ THE SPEC (OPENS IN A NEW TAB)" in hex across a three-word button. Any future screen-reader-only content inside a `.btn-*` depends on that.
- The homepage's middle is one full-bleed 40/60 split band (`protocol-parts.tsx`, DESIGN.md §5 The Split Spec Sheet), added 2026-08-16 on the user's direction. It replaced the four-parts feature scroller, the tap-to-pay video, and the properties bento in one move; the video, `protocol-demo*.tsx`, the `--fig-*` tokens, and the Demo Panel signature are all deleted. It is deliberately outside the page shell — the entry row needs the width — so don't "align" it back to `--page-max`. Each entry's plate is a `thinking-orbs` canvas driven through the library's engine at ~280px, not its `<ThinkingOrb>` component (which ships only 64px and 20px); see `orb-figure.tsx`. The Honest-Network Rule was amended for these plates: abstract orbs are material and pass freely, but `connecting` is a topology and is pinned to the Mints entry alone, where Lightning makes a mesh true. Don't move it to another entry or add a second instance. (The hero used to run `listening` and no longer runs anything — these four plates are the only `thinking-orbs` figures left on the site.)
- **The homepage hero is centred on purpose. Everything else on the site is left-aligned.** This is the one deliberate departure from the editorial register, added 2026-08-14 on the user's direction — do not "restore" it as a consistency fix, and do not spread centring to the sections beneath it. Two things depend on it and move together if it ever reverts: the absence of optical-left corrections (headline −0.058em, deck −0.095em) and the headline's escape from the page shell. The third — "the figure moves off-axis" — was struck 2026-08-16; there is no figure. See the Centred-Hero Exception in DESIGN.md §4.

When in doubt, run `/impeccable critique` against the surface in question before shipping.
